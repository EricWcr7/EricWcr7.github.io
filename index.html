About

I want to work on AI in practice: taking existing models, such as large language models, image models, and classic machine learning, and turning them into working applications that get real tasks done. I want data science and AI in practice to work as two halves of one project. Data science decides what is worth building, prepares the data, and turns the results into decisions. AI in practice builds the system, ships it, and monitors it. The two meet at evaluation, where the system is tested before anyone relies on it. I’m exploring healthcare, public health, and the environment as places to apply this, and I’m open to others.
