Research

Undergraduate Research Student

University of Toronto

Sep 2026 – Present

Toronto, Ontario

“Correlation-Augmented Neural Retrieval - From Hard Filtering to Spectral Diffusion”

Supervised by Prof. Nick Koudas, Department of Computer Science

  • Dense neural retrievers generalize well, but passages from unrelated topics can sit close together in embedding space and crowd results with off-topic matches. The project tests whether adding a sparse graph of corpus term-association estimates to a dense retriever can reduce those off-topic results, recover relevant passages that share no query terms, and keep query cost under control.
  • It plans to compare four ways to inject that signal, running from the title’s hard filtering to spectral diffusion: pruning candidates before scoring, re-scoring by warping the dense similarity metric, multi-hop diffusion over the term-association graph, and contrastive fine-tuning of the embedding model. Planned work also includes an evaluation protocol and an audit of the assumptions behind the theory.
  • I’m one of three undergraduate students on the project, which is in progress.
